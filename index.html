The Slow Ritual

Three steps,
two minutes, every day

Beauty that grows quietly is built on small, consistent moments. This is the Lunavée ritual — simple enough to keep, lovely enough to look forward to.

01 — Cleanse

Wash with petals

Lather the body wash over damp skin. Real petals and niacinamide cleanse gently while they brighten and calm.

02 — Exfoliate

Polish 2–3× a week

Massage the ice-cream scrub in slow circles to lift away dead skin, then rinse to reveal a velvet-smooth surface.

03 — Nourish

Lock in the glow

Smooth on the body lotion while skin is still soft. 5% niacinamide and avocado butter seal in 24-hour hydration.

The Ingredients

Every active, and the science behind it

We formulate by concern,
not by trend

Behind the petals and the slow ritual is serious skincare. Every Lunavée formula starts with a real skin concern, then earns its way back with proven, well-tolerated actives — many of them face-grade. Here's what's doing the work, and why it matters.

01
The all-rounder · in every formula

Niacinamide

Often called the "do-everything" vitamin — and ours is renewable-grade and exceptionally well tolerated. It works on several fronts at once: reinforcing the skin barrier by boosting its own ceramides and lipids, defending against UV, blue light and pollution, evening tone by limiting melanin transfer, refining the look of blemishes, and supporting firmness over time.

BrighteningBarrierAnti-pollutionTexture
02
Body Lotion · SK-INFLUX®

Triple Ceramides

We don't just add one ceramide — we rebuild the barrier the way skin builds it. A precise blend of ceramides 1, 3 and 6-II with cholesterol, free fatty acids and phytosphingosine recreates the same multi-layer "liquid-crystal" structure as skin's own lipids: the anchor (Cer 1) locks the layers together, the guardian (Cer 3) prevents water loss, and the coordinator (Cer 6-II) keeps renewal smooth.

Barrier RepairAnti-moisture-lossCalming
03
Body Wash & Scrub · fresh-preserved

Real Flower Petals

Most "floral" products rely on fragrance alone. We use a fresh-preservation technique that locks real rose, jasmine and camellia petals at their peak — so the actual petal stays intact and active in the gel, unaffected by heat or light. Rose is deeply calming, too: its naturally occurring linalool and geraniol are linked to easing tension and restful sleep, while hydrating and brightening the skin.

SoothingCalmingHydrating
04
Body Lotion · patented signature

Nine-Grade Lotus Water

Our self-developed, patented extract from Taiwanese nine-grade scented lotus — bred over decades and grown pesticide-free. Rich in lotus amino acids, polysaccharides and polyphenols, it delivers antioxidant protection on par with vitamin C, holds moisture in the skin, and helps improve elasticity and tone. It's the same lotus family prized by luxury houses like Fresh and Kenzo.
